Executive Summary

73% of the DTS team uses AI daily, collectively saving 21 hours/week (~967-1,118h/year). The team has identified 9 actionable use cases across knowledge access, workflow automation, testing & quality validation, and RAG-based assistance. Adoption is led by Microsoft Copilot and SiemensGPT (82% each); the biggest growth opportunity is deepening confidence (only 1/11 rate themselves “very confident”) and expanding into GitHub Copilot for the engineering roles. A structured enablement program — hands-on Copilot, prompt engineering and a testing-AI pilot — would unlock the next tier of productivity.
